# Mobile brand and system wallpaper motifs

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool in generate mode. Stylized outline interpretations for wallpaper decoration, with names displayed in the selector.

- [Source atlas](mobile-atlas.png), unchanged, transparent PNG.
- [Preview](mobile-preview.png).

## Exact prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create ONE production sprite atlas of mobile phone brand and operating-system inspired wallpaper motifs, exactly 4 columns by 4 rows, 16 isolated symbols, square canvas about 1536x1536. Actual transparent background. Pure BLACK clean rounded monoline outline ink, transparent interiors, no white fill, no gray shading, no colors, no shadows, no sticker borders, no background. Each emblem centered within its equal grid cell with very generous empty transparent gutters, comparable visual size 200-240px and 7-9px strokes. Every motif fully contained in its own cell, never touches another or a grid boundary. Preserve recognizable iconic silhouettes and simple wordmarks. Exact order left to right, top to bottom: Row1 (1) Apple bitten apple with detached leaf, outlined, (2) Huawei eight-petal fan emblem with detached petals in outline, (3) Xiaomi rounded square containing its iconic outlined MI monogram, (4) Samsung italic SAMSUNG wordmark in a tilted thin elliptical orbit; Row2 (5) OPPO distinctive rounded lowercase oppo wordmark as outline strokes, (6) vivo geometric lowercase vivo wordmark as outline strokes, (7) HONOR crisp capital wordmark as outlined letters, (8) OnePlus simple square outline with 1 and plus at top right; Row3 (9) Meizu stylized geometric MEIZU wordmark as clean line lettering, (10) HarmonyOS infinity-like dual rounded loop emblem with a short underline accent, (11) Android classic friendly robot bust with two antennae and two small eyes, outlined, (12) iOS lowercase i followed by capitals OS in Apple's simple rounded typography as outlined letters; Row4 (13) HyperOS distinctive rounded circular orbit with short HyperOS lettering inside, (14) ColorOS simple clean ColorOS wordmark with small circular O, outlined lettering, (15) OriginOS minimalist square and rounded-circle geometric emblem with a tiny dot accent, (16) One UI two overlapping soft rounded-square interface panels and short One UI lettering. Brand/system names may appear ONLY inside their own emblem as specified, no captions, labels, slogans, watermark or additional words. This is a coherent, elegant, playful outline wallpaper motif family, not a marketing sheet. Generous margins for clean automated cropping. Keep all holes and gaps truly transparent.
