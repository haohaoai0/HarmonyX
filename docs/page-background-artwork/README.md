# Generated wallpaper motifs

52 transparent outline motifs, generated with the built-in imagegen tool in generate mode. The signature set used the application's alternate icons as visual references. The Plants vs. Zombies set was generated from a text description. Source atlases remain unchanged; the prep script trims each isolated cell and preserves alpha, then makes matching black and white ink variants for theme-aware rendering.

All four sets are available in Page background > Individual motifs. Each motif can be selected separately, mixed with any other set, and included in portable .hxb schemes. Full-pattern collections remain available separately.

## Signature atlas prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create one production sprite atlas for subtle app wallpaper motifs, transparent background. Reference images are visual identity references only: the app's blue smiling frog, yellow milk-frog mascot, zombie X alternate icon, bird alternate icon, blossom X alternate icon. Do not edit or replace the originals. Canvas landscape, exactly 4 columns and 3 rows, 12 isolated motifs, evenly spaced regular grid, one fully-contained motif centered in each equal cell with generous transparent gutters. Pure black rounded monoline outline artwork only, transparent interiors (except small black eyes), no white fill, no gray shading, no colors, no scene, no text, no labels, no cell borders, no background, no shadows. Consistent 7-9 px stroke at approximately 1536x1152 canvas; clean readable at 40vp, each silhouette about 210-250px. Order left to right top to bottom: (1) smiling blue-frog mascot face with two bulging eye domes and broad cheeks, (2) same frog sitting on a lily pad, (3) yellow milk-frog mascot bust with its distinctive rounded tall forehead and big eyes, (4) same milk-frog waving, (5) small upward-flying bird matching reference shape, (6) friendly bird with outspread wings, (7) simple double-ribbon geometric X emblem, (8) X intertwined with a single cherry blossom twig, (9) expressive brushstroke-style outlined X, (10) three parallel folded ribbon strips forming X, (11) playful dripping slime X with a little goofy zombie head peeking from its upper right matching reference, (12) lily pad and small blossom. Keep all motifs detached from one another and entirely inside their cell; generous margins.

## Plants vs. Zombies atlas prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Create ONE production sprite atlas for Plants vs. Zombies themed app wallpaper motifs. Transparent background, clean elegant black monoline cartoon doodles, consistent rounded 7-9px outlines, mostly transparent interiors with tiny solid-black pupils only. Exactly 4 columns by 3 rows, 12 separate fully contained motifs, one in the center of each equal grid cell, all same apparent scale, very generous empty transparent gutters. No words, no letters, no logos, no numbers, no borders, no scene, no white fill, no color, no gradients, no texture, no background, no shadows. Landscape canvas roughly 1536x1152. Make the recognizable classic Plants vs. Zombies silhouettes cute and simplified enough to read as small 40vp wallpaper icons. Precise order left to right, top to bottom: 1 Peashooter with round trumpet snout, stalk and two leaves; 2 smiling Sunflower with round face and ring of petals; 3 startled Wall-nut with wavy shell and two oval eyes; 4 Cherry Bomb pair attached by short stem; 5 Snow Pea with trumpet snout and four icy head spikes; 6 Potato Mine with small red-light-shaped stalk on top but drawn monochrome; 7 Chomper open toothy mouth and leafy stalk; 8 Squash with grumpy brow and small stalk; 9 goofy classic zombie head, two large uneven eyes, missing tooth and sparse hair; 10 Conehead Zombie bust with tall traffic cone; 11 Buckethead Zombie bust with bucket and loose tie; 12 small lawn mower side view with handle and two wheels. Keep outlines readable and not overly detailed. All 12 isolated designs remain wholly inside their own cell without touching or crossing cell margins.
