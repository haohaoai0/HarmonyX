# X logo source

- Vector artwork: [X logo late 2025.svg on Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:X_(formerly_Twitter)_logo_late_2025.svg), sourced from X.com and authored by X Corp.
- X brand guide: [X Brand Quick Guide for External Partners](https://about.x.com/content/dam/about-twitter/x/brand-toolkit/x-brand-guidelines.pdf). It specifies the white mark on black or the black mark on white.
- `x-logo-2025-preview.png` is the transparent 960 × 960 raster preview used by `tools/generate_x_alternate_icons.py`. The vector includes the clear space; scaling that square preview to 660 × 660 yields a mark about 52% of the icon width, matching the supplied launcher screenshot.
